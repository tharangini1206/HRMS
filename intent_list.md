# HR Chatbot - Intent List

## 1. Leave Management
- Apply for leave (Casual, Sick, Earned, Maternity, etc.)
- Check leave balance
- View leave history / status
- Cancel leave request
- Leave policy questions
- Leave approval status

## 2. Payslip
- Get latest payslip
- Download payslip for specific month
- Salary breakdown details
- Tax and deduction information

## 3. Attendance
- Check attendance record
- View monthly attendance
- Apply for attendance regularization
- Missing punch / Late coming request
- Attendance report

## 4. Company Policies
- General policy inquiries
- Work From Home (WFH) policy
- Reimbursement policy
- Holiday calendar
- Probation and confirmation policy
- Resignation / Exit process

## 5. General HR Support
- HR contact details
- Employee benefits
- Grievance / Complaint registration
- Internal job opportunities
- Training programs

## 6. Fallback / Others
- Greetings and casual talk
- Out of scope queries
- Request to speak with human HR
